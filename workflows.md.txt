# WORKFLOWS.md

# Blue Hills Camp Hospitality ERP
## Business Workflow Specification

Version: 1.0

---

# PURPOSE

This document describes every business workflow currently used by Blue Hills Camp.

The purpose of the software is to digitize these workflows without fundamentally changing the operational procedures.

Every workflow below must eventually be supported by the Hospitality ERP.

---

# GENERAL PRINCIPLES

• Every transaction must have an audit trail.

• Every workflow should record:
    - User
    - Date
    - Time
    - Status
    - Department

• Managers should approve where required.

• Every workflow should support printing.

• Every workflow should support PDF export.

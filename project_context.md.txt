---

# 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Blue Hills Camp Hospitality ERP is an enterprise hospitality management system being designed specifically for Blue Hills Camp, Zimbabwe.

The objective is NOT to change how Blue Hills Camp operates.

The objective is to digitize the camp's existing operational procedures while improving efficiency, reporting, accountability and management visibility.

The software must replace:

• Paper registers
• Paper purchase orders
• Paper guest records
• Manual calculations
• Excel spreadsheets
• Manual reporting

without disrupting existing business processes.

The final system should become the central operating platform for the entire camp.

---

# 2. BUSINESS BACKGROUND

Blue Hills Camp operates as an integrated hospitality establishment providing:

• Accommodation
• Restaurant
• Bar
• Outdoor Activities
• Conference Facilities
• Weddings
• Team Building
• Corporate Retreats
• School Camps

The camp currently manages operations using:

• Paper forms
• Excel spreadsheets
• Printed manuals
• Manual approvals

Each department maintains its own records independently.

This causes duplicated work and delays in management reporting.

---

# 3. PROJECT GOALS

The software should achieve the following:

Digitize all operational departments.

Provide real-time reporting.

Reduce paperwork.

Reduce duplicate data capture.

Improve guest experience.

Improve inventory control.

Improve procurement accountability.

Improve financial reporting.

Provide management dashboards.

Maintain complete audit trails.

Operate completely without internet.

---

# 4. NON-NEGOTIABLE REQUIREMENTS

## OFFLINE FIRST

This system MUST continue operating even if internet connectivity is unavailable.

Internet is considered OPTIONAL.

All critical operations must function locally.

Examples:

✔ Reservations

✔ Check-in

✔ Check-out

✔ Restaurant POS

✔ Activities

✔ Procurement

✔ Inventory

✔ Finance

✔ Reporting

No module should require cloud services.

---

# LOCAL NETWORK

The system should support multiple computers connected over a Local Area Network (LAN).

Example:

Reception

Restaurant

Kitchen

Accounts

Manager

Stores

Maintenance

All users connect to one local server.

# BACKUPS
